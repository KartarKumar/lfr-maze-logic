LFR Maze — Submission 2 — Motor equations
Modules a,b,c,d,e are active low: 0 = wall or black line.
A = NOT a, B = NOT b, C = NOT c, D = NOT d, E = NOT e (one 74HC04).
The raw module pin is the complement. Do not invert a second time.

LF = B'(D' + E) + B C' (A + D' E)          IN1 left forward
LB = B C + A' B (D + E') + B' D E'         IN2 left backward
RF = B'(D + E') + B (C + A' (D + E'))      IN3 right forward
RB = 0                                     IN4 tied to ground

Dead end A B C is a U-turn: LB=1 and RF=1.
A right turn stops the right wheel: LF=1 and RF=0.
