LFR Maze — Submission 2 — Minimized equations
A,B,C = wall sensors (1 = wall). D,E = line sensors (1 = black).

LEFT     = A'BC + A'BD + A'B E' + D B' E'
RIGHT    = A B C' + B' D' E + C' D' E
STRAIGHT = B' D E + B' D' E' = B'(DE + D'E')
REVERSE  = A B C          (dead end: reverse)

LF = STRAIGHT + RIGHT     (IN1, left forward)
LB = LEFT + REVERSE       (IN2, left backward)
   = B C + A' B D + A' B E' + B' D E'
RF = STRAIGHT + LEFT      (IN3, right forward)
RB = RIGHT + REVERSE      (IN4, right backward)
   = A B + B' D' E + C' D' E

A' means NOT A.
